Overview of the USA breakfast menu

McDonald's breakfast is built around hot, handheld food that is fast to order and easy to eat on the go. The US morning menu centers on McMuffin, McGriddles, biscuit and bagel sandwiches, hotcakes, breakfast burritos, crispy hash browns and a full line of McCafé coffee drinks.

The Egg McMuffin, created in the early 1970s by a Santa Barbara, California franchisee, remains the signature item and helped turn breakfast into a major part of the fast-food day.

SandwichesMcMuffin, McGriddles, biscuits and, in some regions, bagels.
Platters & wrapsBig Breakfast, Big Breakfast with Hotcakes and the Sausage Burrito.
Sweet, sides & drinksHotcakes, hash browns, oatmeal, McCafé coffee, juice and milk.
Good to know: The menu varies by region and by restaurant. Bagels and some biscuit items are regional, and limited-time items come and go. Check the McDonald's app for the exact menu near you.

Breakfast hours and availability

Hours are set by each restaurant, so they differ across the USA. Most locations that serve breakfast start early in the morning and switch to the regular menu in late morning.

Typical weekday endAround 10:30 AM at many locations
Typical weekend endAround 11:00 AM at many locations
24-hour restaurantsUsually start breakfast in the early morning hours
All-day breakfastNot a nationwide standard. Ask your local restaurant.

How to check your local hours

  • Open the McDonald's app, choose your restaurant and view the live menu.
  • Check the restaurant's listing in a maps app for posted hours.
  • Drive-thru, lobby and delivery hours can differ.
  • Hash browns are usually offered only during breakfast hours.
Plan ahead: If you're close to the cut-off time, order a little early so you don't miss breakfast.
